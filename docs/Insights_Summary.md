# Insights Summary (Key Findings)

## RFM Segmentation
- Champions and Loyal customers have the highest average spend per customer.
- Potential segment usually has the largest customer count and contributes high total revenue due to volume.
- At Risk and Hibernating segments indicate churn risk and should be targeted with retention campaigns.

## Cohort Retention
- Retention typically drops after Month 1, highlighting a critical churn window.
- Early cohorts show comparatively stronger long-term retention behavior.

## CLV Proxy (Customer Prioritization)
- CLV proxy score helps prioritize a small set of high-value customers for loyalty and premium engagement.

## Market Basket (Cross-Sell Signals)
- Frequent product pair combinations provide directional insights for cross-selling and bundle promotions.
